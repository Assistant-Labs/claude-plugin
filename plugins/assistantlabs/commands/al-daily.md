description: Who is waiting on you today, and what is broken
---

The daily. One screen, a **queue** — not a scorecard.

Read `weekly-report` for the voice, then send, in this order:

1. **The one thing to do today**, with a tap to call or message.
2. **Anyone waiting for a reply**, oldest first — how long, their name, tap.
   Check the conversation before calling anyone unanswered; a "needs a human"
   flag is not the same as silence.
3. **Any reply window closing today**, where waiting costs money or reach.
4. **Today's and tomorrow's appointments**, and whether they are confirmed.
5. **Anything broken** — a channel down, the agent failing, a payment declined.
6. **Yesterday in raw counts**: enquiries, jobs booked, jobs done.

**Never in a daily:** revenue, churn, percentages, week-on-week changes, charts,
or anything that cannot be acted on today. One day of any number is noise, and
noise is what teaches someone to stop opening it.

**It is allowed to be empty.** If it is all-clear five days running, the
thresholds are wrong — raise them or stop sending it.
