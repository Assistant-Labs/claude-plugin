Build the full picture of `$ARGUMENTS`. Read `customer-conversations` first.

**Before analysing, ask the owner one question: did anything happen with them
outside the chat — a call, a meeting, a message from your own phone?** Ask it
first, not after. A page of analysis built on a missing conversation is waste at
best.

Then gather:

1. **Their record** — contact details, labels, what they buy, since when.
2. **Every conversation**, across every channel, in order. Note who spoke: the
   customer, the AI, or a human from the business.
3. **Any attachment** they sent, described. If the agent could not read an
   image, that question is still open.
4. **Their money** — what they have paid, what is outstanding, what they are on.
5. **Every open task** with their name on it.
6. **What was promised** to them, by anyone, and whether it happened.

Then say, in this order and nothing more:

- **What they want right now**, in their words.
- **What is blocking it**, and whose move it is.
- **Anything the business promised and didn't do.**
- **The next action**, one line, with a draft if it is a message.

Update the record in the same turn — what they want, next action, blocked on,
last human contact — and **link the contact record itself** so they can check
your work in two seconds (`opening-the-app`). The record comes before
the message: it is where a wrong guess is still free to be wrong.

Keep the write-up short. If the history is long, the summary is not.
