description: Who to chase, warmest first
---

Build the lead board. Read `growth-and-leads` first.

1. **Pull every conversation and contact** that is not an existing paying
   customer of the thing they are asking about. Someone who already buys, asking
   about something else, **is a lead** — the warmest kind.
2. **Find each one's last REAL message** — theirs, not ours, not an
   away-message, not a receipt.
3. **Rank by that date.** Nothing else. ≤10 days = P1 now · 11–17 = P1 soon ·
   18–28 = P2 soon · 29+ or never = P2 open.
4. **Drop the ones that aren't leads** — nobody ever wrote, spam, wrong number —
   and say how many you dropped and why.
5. **Read each thread** for what they actually want and what is blocking it.
   Check for anything that happened off-channel before concluding anything.
6. **File or update one task per lead.** One lead, one row, with the warmth date
   in the body. Never a grouped "cold leads ×5" row.
7. **Check what already chases them** (`list_follow_ups`) before drafting
   anything by hand — a hand-written chase on top of a running sequence is two
   messages to one person. If nothing covers a pattern you keep seeing, propose
   a sequence instead of a message; see `growth-and-leads`.
8. **Draft the next message** for the top ones — warm, short, no apology, one
   concrete next step, asking for the artefact rather than the intention.

Then print **the top of that ranking, unedited** — not a shortlist you curated
on the way out. If you filter, say the rule and the count. For each: name, days
since they last spoke, what they want in one line, and the next step.

If a lead is missing that the owner expected, work out which filter removed
them before defending the list.
