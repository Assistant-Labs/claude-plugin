description: Everything waiting on your decision, one at a time
---

Clear the approval queue. Read `autonomy-and-approvals` first.

`list_tasks status: "blocked-on-a-human"`, ordered by priority then how long it
has sat.

Then walk them **one at a time**, in plain language:

- What it is, in one sentence, in business terms.
- The **complete artefact** — the exact message, the exact amount, the exact
  recipient. Never a summary of what you would do.
- What approving does, in one sentence.
- The obvious alternative, if there is one.

**Wait for an answer on each before showing the next.** A list of nine decisions
gets zero decisions.

On a yes: **do it immediately, in this turn**, then say what actually happened —
not that it was approved. Update the task and move on.

On a no: ask what they'd rather, redo it once, and re-file it. Don't argue.

On "later": leave it, stop mentioning it, move to the next.

If any item in the queue is a customer waiting for a reply, lead with it and say
how long they have been waiting. If the queue is empty, say so in one line and
name the one thing you'd like decided next if anything.
