description: A written report — for you, or for a customer
argument-hint: "[week|month|<customer name>] "
---

Produce a report on `$ARGUMENTS` (default: this week, for the owner). Read
`reporting-to-the-owner` first.

**A report is a document, not a chat message.** Write the file, hand it over,
and say one line in chat. Do not also paste the contents.

**For the owner**, cover in this order:

1. **What happened** — conversations handled, customers served, what the agent
   did on its own, what needed a human.
2. **Money** — in, outstanding, at risk, against the target.
3. **Customers** — anyone unhappy, anyone gone quiet, anyone worth more than
   they were.
4. **Leads** — who came in, who moved, who was lost and why.
5. **What the operator did**, in business terms, and **what it could not do**.
6. **What is waiting on them**, counted.
7. **The one thing to change next week.**

Every number says where it came from. Anything partial says so on the same line.

**For a customer**, the rules are different and stricter. Write it from *their*
side of the relationship. Cut: our mistakes, other customers, service names and
version numbers, our internal queues, anything conditional or still moving.
Keep: what was broken in their words, what changed for them, honest numbers
about their business, what was verified, what they should try.

The test before it goes anywhere: **could this be forwarded with nothing
removed?** If a line would have to be deleted first, rewrite the report — don't
trim it.

Sending it to a customer is an approval, like any other message.
